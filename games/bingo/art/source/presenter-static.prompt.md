# Ilustración del presentador estático

Creada con la herramienta integrada `image_gen`, modo de ilustración con referencia de identidad y fondo transparente. El modelo de Blender aprobado sirve como referencia del mismo señor y del bombo; la imagen final tiene acabado artístico 2D. Se utiliza en el selector de TV y como reserva de la partida y los resultados. La portada utiliza una ilustración complementaria del mismo anfitrión, documentada en [su prompt](presenter-cover.prompt.md).

Fuente final: [presenter-static.png](presenter-static.png). Salida optimizada: [presenter-static.webp](../../public/assets/presenter-static.webp).

## Prompt utilizado

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: polished commercial 2D illustration for the static fallback of a family bingo game, shown over an existing dark blue and burgundy theatrical background. Input image: identity and object-layout reference ONLY, not a style reference. Reimagine the exact elderly bingo host shown in the reference as a beautiful professionally hand-painted TWO-DIMENSIONAL illustration. Preserve his recognizable identity: bald round head with silver-gray curly hair at the sides, thick curved gray eyebrows, round black glasses, large kind brown eyes, round peach nose, broad gray moustache, friendly smile, rosy cheeks; red waistcoat over white shirt, red bow tie, dark charcoal trousers and rounded black shoes. Keep his full body on the RIGHT and the golden spherical bingo cage on its stand on the LEFT, entirely visible including shoes and the base. His near hand gently operates the crank and his free hand welcomes the audience with a soft relaxed gesture. Natural charming hands, no tense spread fingers. Style: premium family board-game packaging art, warm gouache and delicate colored-pencil detail, expressive elegant contours, carefully designed silhouettes, rich warm reds and golds, soft painted shadows and highlights, appealing editorial illustration with craftsmanship and warmth. Clearly a flat hand-painted illustration, NOT a 3D render or a screenshot, NOT glossy plastic, NOT a toy figurine. Improve the expressiveness and commercial polish while keeping it clearly the same elderly man and same bingo drum. Ivory balls inside the cage, no readable text or numerals. Transparent alpha background, isolated complete host and drum with comfortable clear margins. No environment, no floor rectangle, no typography, no labels, no watermark, no extra people or props. Keep the composition compact and nearly square so both subjects remain legible when small on a television.
